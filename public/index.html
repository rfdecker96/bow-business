A little about us

Two makers. Lots of bows. 💕

Welcome to Bow Business! We're a husband-and-wife team with a love for creating adorable, handmade bows that add a little extra sweetness to everyday moments.

Every bow is made with care, creativity, and attention to the little details. From playful patterns to pretty colors, we love creating pieces that help your little ones look and feel extra special.

What started as a love for crafting has become something we're excited to share with other families. Every order means so much to us, and we're incredibly thankful for everyone who chooses to support our small business.

The little details

Shopping with confidence 🎀

Answers about our made-to-order bows, shipping, and care.

✂️ Made to order

Each order is made especially for you. Please allow 3–5 business days for processing before shipping.

📦 Shipping

We currently ship within the United States only. Shipping charges are shown during checkout.

💕 Returns & order issues

Returns are accepted for damaged or incorrect items only. If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact us so we can help.

🎀 Bow attachments

We make bows with clips and headbands. Please check the individual product details or ask us which attachment is available for the bow you love.

🌸 Caring for your bows

Handle your handmade bows gently and store them where they won't be crushed. For cleaning questions, contact us with your bow's design so we can give suitable care guidance.

❔ Quick questions

When will my bow be ready?

Our processing time is 3–5 business days before shipping.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not at this time. We ship within the United States only.

Can I return a bow?

Returns are accepted for damaged or incorrect items only.

Can I choose a clip or headband?

Both attachment styles are part of our range. Availability may depend on the bow; please check the listing or ask before ordering.
